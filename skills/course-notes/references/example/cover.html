Plate I · slide 60, with the weights computed

Slide 60 draws one query, teddy bear, wired to a key under every word. This is that picture with the attention weights a toy head computes. The line widths are the weights.

q the query: what teddy bear is looking for
k one key per word: what that word offers to be matched on
w softmax of the scaled dot products; the six weights sum to 1
v the values, averaged with those weights, become the new teddy bear
