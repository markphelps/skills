These notes cover Lecture 1 of CME 295, Transformers & Large Language Models, given on 25 September 2026 syllabus. They follow the lecture's order, cite a slide or the paper for every fact, and spend the most space on four topics that are hard on a first pass: attention, multi-head attention, LLM architecture, and training versus inference.

They are written at level 2 of 5: for a reader with some programming who is new to, or rusty on, linear algebra and probability. Part 0 teaches the math the lecture uses, with small examples on the lecture's own sentence. Each formula is followed by a reading in plain words, and each new term is set in bold where it is defined. A reader comfortable with the math can skip Part 0; each lecture section opens with a "Uses" box saying which background sections it relies on.
